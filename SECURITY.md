# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the [Haarness organization](https://github.com/haarness) that
does not carry its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

Report it privately, by either:

- GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository
  (**Security → Report a vulnerability**), or
- email to **admin@haarness.ai**.

Include what we need to reproduce it: the project and version, the platform, the configuration involved
(redact secrets), and the steps or a proof of concept. Tell us how you would like to be credited.

| Step | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement of your report | 3 business days |
| First assessment (accepted, needs info, or not a vulnerability) | 10 business days |
| Fix or mitigation for accepted high/critical issues | 90 days, usually much sooner |

We will keep you informed, agree a disclosure date with you, and credit you in the advisory unless you
ask us not to. We will not take legal action against research done in good faith within this policy:
stay on your own installations and accounts, do not access or modify other people's data, and give us
reasonable time to fix before disclosure.

Haarness Cloud, the hosted service at haarness.ai, also publishes its disclosure policy at
<https://haarness.ai/security>.
